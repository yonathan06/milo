 # AI Event Video Editor — Business Plan

### The idea
A **video-editing agent that lives inside WhatsApp**. Users send photos and video clips, describe what they want in natural language—“make a 60-second wedding highlight,” “remove the boring parts,” “make a funny version for the family group,” “create three Instagram reels”—and receive finished edits back.

The experience replaces the traditional workflow of **finding an editor → uploading files → briefing them → waiting → reviewing → requesting revisions** with an ongoing conversation:

**Send media → Ask → Receive edit → Chat to revise → Done.**

The long-term vision is to become the **AI video editor people message**, rather than another editing application they need to learn.

---

## Problem & opportunity
Event video is highly valuable but editing remains expensive, slow and inconvenient. At the same time, smartphones have dramatically increased the amount of footage captured at weddings, birthdays, company events, trips and family celebrations—creating far more content than people actually have time to edit.

WhatsApp provides an unusually natural interface for this behavior: more than **3 billion people use WhatsApp globally**, and consumers are already accustomed to sending photos, videos, voice notes and instructions through chat.

Weddings alone provide a strong entry market. About **2 million U.S. couples married in 2025**, generating more than **$100B in wedding spending**. Globally, photography and videography represent a substantial part of wedding-services expenditure; Grand View Research estimates that category at **35.2% of global wedding-services revenue in 2025**.

---

## Target market segments

**1. Weddings — beachhead market**  
Couples, guests, wedding photographers and videographers. Products range from instant social clips to full highlight films, speeches, ceremony edits and multiple vertical reels.

**2. Corporate & professional events**  
Conferences, company parties, product launches, off-sites, real-estate events and community events. Customers need fast highlight videos, speaker clips and social-media content. U.S. meeting and event-related travel spending alone exceeded **$126B in 2024**, indicating the scale of the underlying business-events ecosystem.

**3. Family & social events**  
Birthdays, bar/bat mitzvahs, anniversaries, graduations, vacations, baby events and memorial videos. High-volume, lower-ticket, highly viral consumer use cases.

**4. B2B2C creators & event professionals**  
Photographers, videographers, wedding planners, DJs, venues and agencies use the agent as a production layer—allowing one professional to deliver dramatically more edited content without increasing editing headcount.

---

## Business model

**Consumer / pay per event**
- Quick Reel: **$19–39**
- Event Highlight: **$79–149**
- Wedding/Event Package: **$199–399**
- Add-ons: additional edits, longer films, rush processing, cloud storage, AI restoration and premium templates.

**Professional subscription**
- Solo Creator: **$99–149/month**
- Studio / Event Business: **$299–499/month**
- Enterprise/API: usage-based pricing for agencies, venues and event platforms.

A freemium first edit or watermarked preview can drive WhatsApp-native referrals.

## Revenue potential — illustrative bottom-up case

A credible initial target does not require dominating the editing market.

| Scale | Consumer edits | Pro accounts | Approx. annual revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Early traction | 25K × $75 | 1K × $125/mo | **$3.4M** |
| Growth | 100K × $89 | 5K × $149/mo | **$17.8M** |
| Scale | 500K × $99 | 20K × $199/mo | **$97M** |
| Global platform | 2M × $109 | 75K × $249/mo | **$442M+** |

These scenarios exclude storage, enterprise/API revenue and premium upsells.

For perspective, capturing paid AI-editing orders from just **5% of the roughly 2M annual U.S. weddings** at $199 per wedding would represent approximately **$20M/year from U.S. weddings alone** before professional subscriptions, other event categories or international expansion.

## Why this can win
**No editing software. No timeline. No learning curve.** The interface is conversation.

The moat can become more than the underlying AI models: an event-specific editing system that understands **people, emotional moments, chronology, speeches, music, event types, preferred styles and revision history**. Each interaction teaches the product what the customer means by “more emotional,” “less cheesy,” “focus on the bride,” or “make it feel like a Nike ad.”

The strongest strategic position is not simply **AI video generation**. It is owning the workflow between **raw real-world footage and the finished memories/content people actually want to share**.

### Go-to-market
Launch with weddings, where willingness to pay and emotional value are high. Acquire consumers through photographers, wedding planners, venues and WhatsApp sharing loops; every delivered video becomes an acquisition surface. Then expand horizontally into corporate events and everyday family occasions, while selling professional subscriptions to the creators already supplying the raw footage.

**North-star proposition:**  
**“Send us your footage on WhatsApp. Tell us the video you want. Get it back edited.”**
