 # Milo - AI Event Video Editor

A **video-editing AI agent for events that lives inside WhatsApp**. Users send media files, describe what they want in natural language, and receive finished edits back.
They can even get a shareable link to send to friends to upload their media files for the project.

We are trageting people that want to create videos from an event: weddings, family events, company events, etc.
So instead of hiring a video editor, and doing a back and forth with one to get edited videos, they can just use the AI video agent in whatsapp.
