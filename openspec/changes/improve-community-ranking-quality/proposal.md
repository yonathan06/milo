# Proposal

## Why

Authorized live run `1c21ed18-82b0-4e0a-ba20-e5ad35a96460` produced ten identical 100/100 scores, including title/boilerplate-only evidence and a wedding thread in a broader city subreddit, while excluding two evidenced public wedding forums. Forty-six triage records were deferred and four qualifications failed citation validation; the completed shell fix protects storage/trust boundaries but does not establish assessment quality.

## What Changes

- Preserve title, description, discussion and community-description provenance separately; remove generic platform discovery boilerplate from active inference input without erasing source audit.
- Require evidence about the canonical community before ranking it; a relevant thread or topical title cannot establish the parent community's membership, demand, activity volume or demographics. Insufficient community context remains needs-evidence rather than a fabricated perfect score or audience rejection.
- Accept ordinary public forum/peer-participation wording without requiring one fixed phrase, while continuing to reject articles, vendors, directories and community names without actual participation evidence.
- Make triage candidate/ID associations explicit in bounded structured output; align citation cardinality and unknown-claim instructions across triage/classification/qualification. Add safe citation-rejection categories without repairing model output, guessing IDs, dropping invalid claims or replaying paid calls.
- Enforce supported dimension/claim scope in qualification, preserving the frozen 70/30 rubric, supported-weight normalization, partial coverage and low confidence for provisional evidence. Do not manufacture score diversity with arbitrary caps or penalties.
- Reuse sufficient scoped community evidence before bounded recovery; expose evidence insufficiency and safe rejection categories in existing stage/UI projections.
- Add authored, sanitized live-shaped regressions to shared native-provider/pipeline/safety/persistence/UI scenarios, separate from the frozen healthy benchmark. No harvested member passages or real provider calls in tests.
