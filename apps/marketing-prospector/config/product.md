# Milo — product brief (used as LLM context)

Milo is an AI video editor that lives inside WhatsApp, built for events.

How it works:
1. The user sends Milo photos and video clips on WhatsApp (many files at once).
2. They describe the edit in plain language ("make a 60-second recap", "3 Instagram reels", "funny version for the family group").
3. Milo sends the finished videos back in the chat; they can ask for revisions by chatting.
4. Milo can create a **shareable upload link** — the host sends it to guests/friends/colleagues, everyone uploads their footage, and Milo edits it all together.

Who benefits most:
- People who just had (or are about to have) an event and have lots of footage scattered across many phones: couples, wedding guests, party hosts, parents, team leads / event organizers.
- Event professionals (photographers, videographers, planners, DJs, venues) who want to deliver more video content without editing.

Strong buying signals in a community:
- Members ask how to collect photos/videos from guests, ask for editing apps, recap/highlight videos, reels, "same-day edits", or complain about editing cost/time.
- Members regularly plan or run events (weddings, parties, conferences, celebrations).

Pricing: freemium first edit; paid per event ($19–399) and pro subscriptions.
Website: milo landing page (segments: weddings, company-events, family-events, friends-parties).
